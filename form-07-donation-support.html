Where Your Support Goes

Sporting Pearls Football Academy is a community institution in Mtwapa, Kilifi County. Support directed at player scholarships keeps talented children in training regardless of their family's ability to pay; equipment support equips every squad; girls' football grows the Sporting Pearls Féminin pathway; and community programmes extend the academy's reach beyond the pitch.
