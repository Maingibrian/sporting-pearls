Form 09: Media, Photo & Video Consent

Because the academy works with young players, this consent needs particularly careful handling. This form lets you allow — or refuse — each type of media use separately, so one decision never silently covers another. Your child's welfare comes before social-media engagement, always.

Step 01 of 04

Player & Guardian Details

The player this consent applies to, and the parent or guardian legally able to grant it.

🛡 Why This Form Exists

Before any identifiable image of a young player is published, Sporting Pearls requires recorded parental consent and clear rules covering who may photograph players, where images may be taken, who can access them, and where they may be published. Images are never used in a manner that compromises a child's dignity, safety or privacy.

Used for consent records and any future withdrawal requests.
Step 02 of 04

Programme / Team

Tell us which squad or programme the player belongs to, so consent records match our squad registers.

Only if the player has completed Form 01 registration.

How We Handle Player Images

Only authorised academy personnel and approved photographers may photograph or film players, during official training, fixtures and academy events. Access to images is restricted to authorised staff, and every publication decision follows the Academy Safeguarding Policy. When publishing, we follow strict rules on naming, tagging and identifying information for minors.

Step 03 of 04

Consent Channels — Decide Each One Separately

Each channel below is an independent decision. Allowing one channel never implies allowing another. Choose Allow or Do Not Allow for every channel.

1. Academy Website

Match reports, squad features and stories published on the official Sporting Pearls website.

2. Academy Social Media

Official academy accounts on platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, X and TikTok — managed under our social-media procedures, with welfare placed before engagement.

3. Academy Publications

Newsletters, programmes, annual reports and printed materials produced by the academy for its community.

4. Promotional Materials

Posters, banners, flyers, brochures and campaign materials used to promote the academy and its programmes.

A Note on Refusing

Choosing Do Not Allow on any channel — or on all of them — will never affect your child's playing time, selection, or standing at the academy. Consent is about media, nothing else.

Step 04 of 04

Withdrawal Rights & Declaration

Review your decisions and sign the declaration.

↩ How to Withdraw Consent

You may withdraw this consent at any time by giving written notice to the academy office (info@sportingpearlsacademy.org / pearlssporting@gmail.com) or directly to the Welfare & Safeguarding Lead. Withdrawal applies to all future publications; where technically possible, previously published images will also be removed. Withdrawal is recorded against this consent form and requires no explanation.
