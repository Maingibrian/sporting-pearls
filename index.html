About Us

|

Founded in 2020, Sporting Pearls Football Academy has grown into a beacon of hope and opportunity for young footballers in Kilifi County. We believe that football is more than just a game — it's a vehicle for personal growth, community development, and social transformation.

Our qualified coaches and mentors work tirelessly to develop not just skilled players, but confident, disciplined, and respectful young people who are prepared for success both on and off the pitch.
