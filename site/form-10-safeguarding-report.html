Child safety is our absolute priority. Use this secure form to report any incident, welfare concern, suspicion of abuse, neglect, bullying, harassment, or unethical conduct. Reports are treated with the highest degree of confidentiality.

Reporter Identification & Confidentiality

🛡️ Confidential Reporting

You may submit this report completely anonymously. If you choose to give your details, they are visible only to the academy's Designated Safeguarding Officer (DSO), on a strict need-to-know basis. Please understand: where a child is at risk, the DSO may be required by law to share relevant information with statutory authorities (such as the Children's Officer or the Police). Reports made in good faith are handled seriously and discreetly, and retaliation against anyone raising a genuine concern is a disciplinary offence at the academy.

While you type, and if the report cannot be sent right away, a copy is kept on this device only — for up to 48 hours — and deleted once delivered or expired. Please use a device you trust. How we handle your information.
