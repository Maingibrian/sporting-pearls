Beyond the Pitch

Great players. Greater people.

We care about school grades as much as goals. Every Sporting Pearls player trains on the pitch and grows in the classroom — a structured life-skills curriculum runs alongside every squad's football week.

  • 01

    Academic Commitment

    Attendance monitoring, homework check-ins and tutoring partnerships.

  • 02

    Time Management

    Balancing training, matches, school and family — punctuality first.

  • 03

    Goal Setting & Vision

    Season targets, life ambitions and individual development plans.

  • 04

    Digital Literacy & Safety

    Responsible social media use and protecting online reputation.

  • 05

    Communication & Leadership

    Confident voices on the pitch, in class and in the community.

  • 06

    Personal Responsibility

    Integrity, healthy habits and accountability for choices.

About Us

|

Founded in 2020, Sporting Pearls Football Academy has grown into a beacon of hope and opportunity for young footballers in Kilifi County. We believe that football is more than just a game — it's a vehicle for personal growth, community development, and social transformation.

Our qualified coaches and mentors work tirelessly to develop not just skilled players, but confident, disciplined, and respectful young people who are prepared for success both on and off the pitch.
