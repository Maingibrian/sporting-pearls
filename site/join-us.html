02 · Onboarding Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions.

What age groups are accepted into the academy?

We enroll players aged 7 to 19+ across six structured squads: U9 Discovery, U13 Intelligence, U15 Performance, U17 Transition, U19/Senior, and Sporting Pearls Féminin (The Butterflies).

What should players bring to their trial session?

Trialists must bring standard football boots (moulded studs or turf trainers), shin guards, black shorts/socks, a water bottle, and a copy of their birth certificate or school ID card.

How are academic commitments monitored?

Football is a platform for life. We review school report cards every term and provide study hall support to ensure sports participation never compromises academic performance.

Where do training sessions take place?

Academy sessions take place at Mtwapa Central Academy Grounds and Mtwapa Primary School Field in Kilifi County. Detailed session schedules are shared upon registration confirmation.
